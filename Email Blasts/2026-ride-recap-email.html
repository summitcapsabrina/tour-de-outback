174 Riders, Two Nights of Music, and a Forecast That Missed

By Dave Tucker

This year's Oregon Tour de Outback pulled in 174 registered riders, up from 161 in 2025, with 145 rolling out from the Lake County Fairgrounds behind an Oregon State Police escort. They came from six states for five routes through the high desert, two nights of live music, a bobcat sighting, and a forecast that had everyone bracing for the worst right up until ride day arrived milder than anyone expected.

If you rode it, this is your souvenir. If you didn't, here's what you missed, and why June 26, 2027 is worth marking on the calendar now.

Why Your Registration Matters

Every entry fee helps fund the same all-volunteer Search and Rescue team keeping riders safe out there, the crew that answers search and rescue calls across Lake County the other 364 days of the year.
